Eye gestures detected

Detects: blink · double blink · long blink (eyes shut 1–2 s) · left / right wink · looking left, right, up or down (held ½ s) · wide eyes · squint. Normal is about 12–20 blinks a minute. A much higher rate can go with stress or dry eyes, and long closures can mean tiredness.

Signs being measured

Alertness this session

Live signs: eyes-closed time (PERCLOS), blink length, long closures, micro-sleeps (eyes shut 2 s+), yawns, head nodding off, and a glazed stare. Face signs that work even on a photo: hanging eyelids, dark circles, red eyes, and drooping mouth corners. Eye shapes differ between people, so press Calibrate while you're fully awake and eyelid droop will be measured against your own eyes. Dark circles can also be genetic, and redness can come from allergies or screens. This is an estimate, not a sleep test. If you're very sleepy most days despite 7–9 hours in bed, talk to a doctor (conditions like sleep apnoea are common and treatable).

BHAAV · they are saying

Welcome to BHAAV 👋

BHAAV reads what a face and hands are showing (emotions, eye and head gestures, hand signs, tiredness) and can speak for someone who can't.

🔒Private by design. Video is processed on this device and never uploaded or saved.
💬Honest about limits. It reads expressions, not minds, and always shows why it thinks something.

Set up for a good reading

💡Light in front of you, not behind you. A window behind you makes your face too dark.
📏Sit about an arm's length away, with your face and upper chest in view.
😐When the camera starts, hold a relaxed face for 2 seconds. That calibrates it to your face.
👓Glasses, beards and masks are fine. BHAAV adapts, and you can set them in Settings.

What would you like to do?

You can switch at any time with the buttons at the top, or keys 1 · 2 · 3.

Getting the best reading

Watch the Signal bars at the top. When something is wrong, a tip appears on the camera view (too dark, too far, turned away). Hold signs still for about a second; the bar under the sentence fills as it gets ready to type. Press ⏸ whenever you want to move freely without typing anything.
