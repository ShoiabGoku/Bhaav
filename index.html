Emotion readings come from 52 facial-muscle movements (brows, eyelids, cheeks, lips, jaw). They describe what the face is showing, not what a person feels inside, and they can't diagnose anxiety or any other condition. Calibrate your neutral face for more accurate readings.

Eye gestures detected

Detects: blink · double blink · long blink (eyes shut ≥ 1 s) · left / right wink · looking left, right, up or down (held ½ s) · wide eyes · squint. Normal is about 12–20 blinks a minute. A much higher rate can go with stress or dry eyes, and long closures can mean tiredness.

Head gestures

Nod = yes, shake = no. Both can be spoken automatically when eye/head commands are on.

Built-in signs: 👍 👎 ✊ 🖐 ☝️ ✌️ 🤟 👌 🤙 🤘 👉 🤏, three, four, L-shape and pinky. Hold a sign steady to type its phrase. You can change what each sign says under Settings.

Leave a box empty to ignore that trigger. Special actions: [speak] speaks the sentence, [undo] removes the last word, [clear] clears it.

BHAAV runs Google MediaPipe face and hand models in your browser (WebAssembly + GPU). Camera frames are never uploaded or stored. Only your settings and custom signs are saved, in this browser. Emotion and stress readings are estimates from facial movement and are not medical or psychological assessments.
